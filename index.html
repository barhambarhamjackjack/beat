Bobby

Your local guardian

Bobby maps what's really happening on the streets of Kensington & Chelsea — Notting Hill to World's End. Neighbours report what they see, other neighbours corroborate it, and the result is shown as heat you can read at a glance.

Bobby is not an emergency service. Always call 999 in an emergency, or 101 to report a crime to the police. Reporting something on Bobby does not report it to anyone in authority.

How it works

  • Report in under a minute. Category, location, a sentence of detail. Always shown anonymously.
  • Every report is moderated. Nothing appears on the map until a human has reviewed it.
  • Neighbours corroborate. Three independent confirmations mark a report verified.
  • Heat, not pins. The map shows where problems concentrate, weighted towards the last 48 hours.

Reporting incidents, not people

Bobby exists to describe what happened where. It is not for describing, naming or locating individuals. Reports containing names, addresses, postcodes, vehicle registrations or contact details are automatically withheld for review, and removed. See the community rules.
