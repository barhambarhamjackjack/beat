Delete my data

You can erase your account and everything Bobby holds about you yourself, in about ten seconds. No email, and no waiting.

In the app: open Bobby → You tab → Delete my data → confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account itself, and the link between it and your Apple ID — Bobby's access to your Apple ID is revoked at the same time
  • Every report you submitted, published or pending, and every photo and video attached to them
  • Every confirmation you gave and every reaction you left
  • Every comment you wrote
  • Every content flag you raised
  • Your block list, your saved places, your chosen area and your alert preferences
  • Your push notification tokens, on every device
  • Any position stored for nearby alerts

This is immediate and irreversible. Deleting a report removes it from everyone's map, and takes its comments and confirmations with it — including other people's.

Waiting list postcode districts

Waiting list sign-ups are not tied to your account, so they are not covered by the in-app button. Email jack@barhamfamily.com with the district and it will be removed.

If you have already deleted the app

Deleting the app does not delete your account — sign in again on any device and use the in-app button, or email us from the address associated with your Apple ID and we will do it for you.

Other data rights

Access, correction, portability and objection requests can be made to jack@barhamfamily.com and are answered within one month, as UK GDPR requires. See the privacy policy.
