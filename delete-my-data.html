Delete my account

You can erase everything Patch holds about you yourself, in about ten seconds. No email, no waiting, no support ticket.

In the app: open Patch → You tab → Delete my account → confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your Patch account
  • Every report you submitted, published or pending
  • Every corroboration you gave
  • Every content flag you raised
  • Your block list
  • Your push notification token
  • Patch's access to your Apple ID, revoked with Apple directly

This is immediate and irreversible. Your reports disappear from everyone's map, which also means the heat they contributed goes with them.

Waiting list emails

Borough waiting list sign-ups are not tied to your device, so they are not covered by the in-app button. Email jack@barhamfamily.com from the address you signed up with and it will be removed.

If you have already deleted the app

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Reinstall and sign in to use the button above, or email jack@barhamfamily.com from the address on your account and we will do it for you within one month, as the law requires.

Removing Patch from your Apple ID

Deleting your account revokes Patch's access automatically. You can also check or remove it yourself at any time under iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Other data rights

Access, correction, portability and objection requests can be made to jack@barhamfamily.com and are answered within one month, as UK GDPR requires. See the privacy policy.
