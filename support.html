Support

Patch is made by one person. Emails are read by a human, usually the same day.

Email: jack@barhamfamily.com

Response time: within one working day. Content concerns are dealt with within 24 hours, seven days a week.

Reporting content

The fastest route is in the app: open any report, tap “···”, then Report this incident. Choose a reason and it goes straight into the moderation queue. If three people independently flag the same report, it comes off the map immediately.

If a report identifies you or someone you know, email us and it will be removed as a priority — you do not need the app to do this.

Common questions

My report hasn't appeared

Every report is reviewed by a human first. You'll see it on your own map marked In review until then. Most are approved within the hour.

What does “verified” mean?

Three different people confirmed they saw the same thing. It does not mean the incident has been confirmed by the police or by us.

Why only Kensington & Chelsea?

A crowd-sourced map is useless until enough neighbours are using it, so Patch opens one borough at a time. Register interest in the app and yours moves up the queue.

Do I need an account?

Not to read the map. You do to report, corroborate, flag or block — that is what lets us moderate properly and stop people who abuse it. Sign in with Apple is the only method, and you can hide your email.

Can I delete what I've posted?

Yes — You → Delete my account in the app removes everything. See Delete my account.

Is Patch linked to the police?

No. Patch is independent, and reporting here does not notify anyone in authority. Call 999 in an emergency or 101 to report a crime.
