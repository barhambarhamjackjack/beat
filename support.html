Reporting content

The fastest route is in the app: open any report or comment, tap “···”, then Report. Choose a reason and it goes straight into the moderation queue. If three people independently flag the same thing, it comes off the map immediately.

If something identifies you or someone you know, email us and it will be removed as a priority — you do not need the app to do this.

Common questions

My report hasn't appeared

Every report is screened before it goes on the map. You will see it on your own feed marked In review until then. Most are through in a minute or two; anything the automated step is unsure about waits for a person.

What does “verified” mean?

Enough different people independently confirmed they saw the same thing. It does not mean the incident has been confirmed by the police or by us. The number needed depends on the area — three across a London borough, fewer across a handful of villages, because three independent witnesses to the same thing is a fair test in one and an impossible one in the other.

Can I use Bobby without an account?

You can read the map and the feed without signing in. Contributing — reporting, confirming, commenting, flagging, blocking — needs a Sign in with Apple account, because those actions have to be accountable. What you post is still shown anonymously; nobody is ever told who wrote what.

Can I post a photo of a number plate?

Yes. Photographs of what happened are welcome, faces and plates included — that is often the most useful thing a report carries. What is not allowed is naming or locating a person: no names, phone numbers, email addresses, handles, full postcodes or home addresses.

Why only these areas?

A crowd-sourced map is useless until enough neighbours are using it, so Bobby opens one area at a time. Put your postcode district on the waiting list in the app — that is all that gets stored — and you will be told when it opens.

Why is the police data two months old?

That is how the Home Office publishes it. It is the shape of an area over time, not what happened last night, and it lives behind its own toggle for exactly that reason. Every screen that shows it says which month it is.

I get alerts on one phone and not the other

Alerts go to every device you have signed in on and enabled them on. If one has gone quiet, open Bobby on it and check Alerts; if that does not fix it, email us.

Can I delete what I've posted?

Yes — any report of your own can be deleted from its “···” menu, and You → Delete my data removes your account and everything tied to it. See Delete my data.

Is Bobby linked to the police?

No. Bobby is independent, and reporting here does not notify anyone in authority. Call 999 in an emergency or 101 to report a crime. The recorded-crime layer is published open data, not a connection to any force.
