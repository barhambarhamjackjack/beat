Community rules

1. Report incidents, never people

Describe what happened and where. Do not include names, physical descriptions of individuals, vehicle registration numbers, addresses, postcodes, phone numbers, email addresses or social media handles. Reports that identify a private individual are removed.

2. Only report what you saw

First-hand accounts, or close to it. Rumour and speculation distort the map and can seriously harm real people and real businesses.

3. No abuse, harassment or hate

No threats, no slurs, and nothing targeting anyone by race, religion, nationality, sex, gender identity, sexuality or disability.

4. Nothing unlawful

Do not post content that is defamatory, that identifies someone protected by a court order, that prejudices active legal proceedings, or that breaks any other law.

5. No spam or manipulation

One corroboration per person. Do not submit false reports, inflate corroborations, or use Beat to promote anything.

Moderation and enforcement

  • Every report is reviewed by a moderator before it appears on the map.
  • Any user can flag any report from the “···” menu on it.
  • Flagged content is reviewed within 24 hours. Content flagged independently by three people is removed from the map immediately, pending that review.
  • Any user can block any reporter, hiding all of their content from them.
  • Accounts that repeatedly break these rules are permanently barred from contributing. Barring is applied to the account, not the phone.
  • Repeated breaches result in a permanent bar.
