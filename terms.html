Bobby is not an emergency service

Call 999 in an emergency and 101 to report a crime to the police. Posting on Bobby does not notify the police or any authority, and no one is monitoring the app for emergencies. Never rely on Bobby for your safety.

Community rules

1. Never name or locate a person

Describe what happened and where. Do not post anyone's name, phone number, email address, social media handle, full postcode or home address — anything a stranger could use to find or contact them. Reports that name or locate a private individual are removed.

Photographs of what happened are fine, faces and number plates included. A face and a plate are part of what happened; a name, a phone number and an address are who somebody is. The failure mode this rule exists to prevent is a stranger turning up at a door, and that needs an address, not a photograph.

2. Only report what you saw

First-hand accounts, or close to it. Rumour and speculation distort the map and can seriously harm real people and real businesses.

3. No abuse, harassment or hate

No threats, no slurs, and nothing targeting anyone by race, religion, nationality, sex, gender identity, sexuality or disability.

4. Nothing unlawful

Do not post content that is defamatory, that identifies someone protected by a court order, that prejudices active legal proceedings, or that breaks any other law.

5. No spam or manipulation

One confirmation per person per report. Do not submit false reports, inflate confirmations, or use Bobby to promote anything.

Moderation and enforcement

  • Every report is screened before it appears on the map — automatically first, and by a person for anything the automated step is not confident about.
  • Comments are not held for review before they appear. Reporting and blocking are what govern them, and both are one tap away.
  • Any user can flag any report or comment from the “···” menu on it.
  • Flagged content is reviewed within 24 hours. Content flagged independently by three people is hidden immediately, pending that review.
  • Any user can block any contributor, hiding all of their content.
  • Repeated breaches result in a permanent bar.

Accuracy, and what Bobby does not promise

Bobby shows what members of the public have reported. Reports are screened for rule-breaking, but they are not verified as fact, and “verified” in the app means only that enough other users independently said they saw the same thing. How many is enough varies with the size of the area. Recorded-crime data shown on the map comes from the Home Office, runs roughly two months in arrears, and is not a description of anything happening now.
