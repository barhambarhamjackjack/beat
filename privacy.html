Privacy policy

Last updated 10 August 2026

Beat is designed to need as little about you as possible. You can read the map without signing in at all. Contributing needs a Sign in with Apple account — and even then, your reports are shown anonymously and no tracking follows you across other apps or websites.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Jack Barham, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom, contactable at jack@barhamfamily.com. This policy is written to meet the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What signing in does not do

Sign in with Apple tells Beat your account identifier and an email address. It does not give Beat access to your contacts, your other apps, your purchases, or anything else in your Apple account. If you choose “Hide My Email”, Beat never learns your real address — and since Beat does not send marketing email, that costs you nothing.

What Beat does not collect

  • No name, date of birth, phone number or postal address
  • No contacts, photos or microphone access
  • No advertising identifier, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics SDKs and no third-party advertising
  • Location is never collected in the background — only while the app is open, and only when you ask for it

What is public

Once a moderator approves it, a report's category, title, description, area and coordinates are visible to everyone using Beat. Your identity is not attached and is never shown — not your name, not your email, and not an account identifier. Other users cannot tell which reports came from the same person; the app is never sent that information. Please treat anything you write in a report as public and permanent — do not include information that identifies you or anyone else.

Who your data is shared with

Beat runs on Supabase (database and hosting) and uses Expo's push notification service to deliver alerts. Map tiles are served by CARTO using OpenStreetMap data; requesting a map tile discloses your IP address to CARTO in the ordinary course of loading the map. The map’s Police layer requests published Home Office crime statistics from data.police.uk, which likewise discloses your IP address to that service; the request asks only for a fixed borough boundary and a month, and carries nothing about you, your location or your account. Beat does not sell data, and shares nothing for advertising.

Beat may disclose information where legally required, for example in response to a valid court order or a police request that meets the statutory conditions.

How long data is kept

  • Published reports — kept indefinitely as part of the public record of the map, unless you ask for erasure
  • Rejected or removed reports — kept for 90 days for moderation audit, then deleted
  • Waiting list emails — until Beat launches in that borough, or you ask us to remove you
  • Push tokens — until you disable notifications, sign out, or delete your account
  • Saved places — until you remove the place or delete your account
  • Your account and the Apple refresh token — until you delete your account, which erases both immediately

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, or portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent for location or notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

To exercise any of these, use the in-app route: open Beat, go to You → Delete my account. It removes your account and everything in it immediately, revokes Beat's access to your Apple ID, and requires no email to us.

You can also email jack@barhamfamily.com. Requests are answered within one month, as the law requires. See Delete my account for detail.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Beat is not directed at children and is age-rated accordingly on the App Store. It is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, contact us and it will be deleted.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in an access-controlled database with row-level security, so a device can only read published reports plus its own pending ones. No system is perfectly secure; please do not put sensitive information in a report.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app before they take effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
